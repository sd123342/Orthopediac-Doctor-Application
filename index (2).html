About Dr. Patil

Care built around your recovery, not just your diagnosis

Dr. Shailesh Patil has spent over eighteen years treating fractures, joint disorders and sports injuries, with a special interest in knee and hip replacement and arthroscopic surgery.

His approach starts with the least invasive option that will get a patient back to full function, and he takes time to walk through every choice so patients understand exactly what to expect before, during and after treatment.
